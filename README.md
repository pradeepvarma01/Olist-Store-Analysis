# 🛍️ Olist Store Analysis – Power BI

## 📊 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing e-commerce sales data from the **Olist Store**, providing insights into sales performance, customer behavior, and delivery efficiency. Built using **Power BI**, the dashboard helps identify key business trends, improve operations, and enhance customer experience through data visualization.

## 🎯 Objectives
- To analyze sales, profit, and order trends across years and quarters.  
- To study customer payment behavior and review scores.  
- To evaluate delivery performance and its impact on customer satisfaction.  
- To identify top-performing categories and payment methods.

## 🧩 Key Insights
- **Total Sales:** 16,008,872 and **Total Profit:** 2,417,228, indicating strong business growth.  
- **Total Orders:** 96,475 from **99,441 unique customers**, showing a broad customer base.  
- **Payment Trends:** 77% of payments occur on weekdays; weekends account for 23%.  
- **Credit Card Dominance:** Most 5-star reviews are linked to credit card payments (43,791 orders).  
- **Average Delivery vs Review:** Faster deliveries correlate with higher review scores — deliveries dropped from 21 to 11 days as ratings improved from 1 to 5.  
- **Yearly Orders:** 2018 recorded the highest order volume (52,778).  
- **Category Insights:** Average delivery time for Pet Shop and Bebes products is around 11 days, while Audio products take longer (13 days).

## 📈 Tools & Techniques
- **Data Visualization:** Power BI  
- **Data Preparation:** Power Query (M code)  
- **Data Modeling:** Star schema with date, customer, and product dimensions  
- **KPIs:** Total Sales, Profit, Orders, Unique Customers, Avg Delivery Days, Review Scores  
- **Charts Used:** Bar charts, Donut charts, Pie charts, Line charts  

## 🧠 Conclusion
The Olist Store Analysis dashboard provides a complete view of **sales performance, delivery efficiency, and customer satisfaction**. It highlights that **faster delivery leads to better reviews**, and **credit card payments dominate high-rated transactions**, offering actionable insights for operational improvement.
